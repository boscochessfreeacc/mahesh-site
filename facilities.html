Innovation Infrastructure

Engineering Fabrication Centre


The Engineering Fabrication Centre at Bharat College of Technology provides students with practical exposure to modern manufacturing, prototyping, and industrial design workflows.


The facility includes advanced additive manufacturing systems, CNC machining equipment, laser cutting tools, embedded systems workstations, and rapid prototyping laboratories that support interdisciplinary engineering projects.


Students use the centre for:

  • 3D printing and rapid prototyping
  • Mechanical fabrication projects
  • Robotics and automation development
  • Industrial product design
  • Research and innovation activities
  • Technical competitions and hackathons

The centre promotes hands-on learning, collaborative problem solving, and industry-oriented technical experience that prepares students for real-world engineering environments.
