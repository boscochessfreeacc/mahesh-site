Visit the Campus

We encourage prospective students to visit the campus before applying. CISH holds four days, per academic year.

Saturday 11 October 2025

Saturday 15 November 2025

Saturday 28 February 2026

Saturday 25 April 2026

Each open day includes talks from the President and senior faculty information sessions for each school tours of the campus and residential facilities and a chance to talk to students. We also provide a lunch in the main dining hall. You need to register in advance. There are limited places available.

Individual Campus Visits

If you cannot attend an open day you can arrange an individual visit. Our Admissions team will try to connect you with a student in your area of interest for an informal tour and conversation. To arrange a visit contact the Undergraduate Admissions Office.

Graduate Open Events

We hold open events on the first Wednesday of November and March each year. If you are an applicant you can join us virtually.

Finding Us

By Rail

Crestfield station has trains from London Paddington and Oxford. The main campus is an eight-minute walk from the station.

By Road

The campus is one mile from junction 14 of the M4 motorway. Limited visitor parking is available; it is an idea to book in advance.

By Air

Heathrow Airport is 35 minutes from Crestfield by coach. Oxford Airport offers aviation facilities 25 minutes from campus.

Write to Us

You can write to us at the campus address. If you address your letter to a person we will forward it to them quickly.
